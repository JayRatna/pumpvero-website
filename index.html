Sample data

01 / 08

  1. Attendant

    Start the shift

    The attendant picks the nozzles, and the opening readings fill in by themselves and cannot be changed.

  2. Attendant

    Closing readings

    The attendant types the closing meter readings and testing litres, and the app works out the litres sold.

  3. Assistant Manager

    Count the cash

    The Assistant Manager counts the cash and enters UPI, card and bank payments, and the app shows Balanced, Shortage or Excess.

  4. Manager

    Approve and lock the day

    The Manager approves or rejects each shift and locks the day, and then only you can open it again.

  5. Manager

    Dip and density

    The Manager types the dip, hydrometer reading and temperature, and the app works out the litres from the tank's dip chart and the density at 15 °C.

  6. Manager

    Scan the tanker invoice

    The Manager scans the invoice QR, and the app fills in the invoice number, date, lorry, litres and total.

  7. Manager

    Check the tanker

    With a dip before and after unloading, the app checks the litres against the invoice.

  8. Owner

    Yesterday's sale by breakfast

    Each morning you see yesterday's fuel sales, and today's sale as each shift ends.

  • Who owes you

    Credit customers have limits and a last-paid date, and the app shows who is overdue.

  • Each day keeps its own fuel rates

    A new petrol or diesel price does not change earlier days.

  • Your own data

    Your data is kept separate from other pump businesses.

  • Who changed what

    After a shift is submitted, the attendant cannot edit it, and every correction is saved with the name of the person who made it.
